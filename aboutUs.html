About Us

The people and the place behind every bottle.

Our Story

Velvet Wine began as a small family project in Hawke's Bay. We wanted to make wine that tastes of the place it comes from, and we still make it the same way: slowly, by hand and with care.

Our History

What started with a few rows of vines has grown season by season. Each harvest has taught us something new, and we have kept the methods that work and improved the ones that do not.

Our Mission & Goals

Grow

Look after the land so our vines stay healthy for many years to come.

Craft

Pick, press and age every wine with patience, never rushing the process.

Share

Bottle wine we are proud of and share it with friends, old and new.

Our Vineyards

Our vines grow on sunny Hawke's Bay slopes, with warm days and cool nights that give the grapes their flavour. Every row is pruned and picked by hand.
