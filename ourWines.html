Our Wines

From the vine to the bottle, see how we make our wine.

Growing

Our vines grow on sunny Hawke's Bay slopes. We prune and care for every row by hand.

Harvesting

Grapes are picked by hand at dawn, when they are cool and ripe, then sorted at the winery.

Ageing

The wine rests in oak barrels for months so the flavours can soften and grow.

Bottling

When it is ready, we bottle, cork and label each wine by hand before it is sold.

Wine Making

Making wine is a slow process and we never rush it. It starts in the vineyard, where healthy vines give us the best fruit. After harvest the grapes are pressed and the juice is left to ferment. Then the wine is aged, tasted often and bottled only when we think it is ready.
